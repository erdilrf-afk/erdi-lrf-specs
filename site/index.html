ERDI laser rangefinder module dataset

59 modules and single-point LiDAR sensors, published as JSON and CSV, where every range figure keeps the manufacturer's own test condition — target size, reflectance, visibility. Values are transcribed, never extrapolated: where no condition is published the field stays empty.

Why the condition matters

Two datasheets that both say "20 km" are not comparable until you know what each was pointed at. A 23 km row and a 3 km row can be the same wavelength, the same eye-safety argument and the same interface — the difference is aperture, pulse energy, mass, and the target the figure was measured against.

Example row: LRF0105C — ≥3,000 m against a 2.3 × 2.3 m reference target at 30 % reflectance and ≥5 km visibility; ≥1,500 m against a human-size 0.5 × 1.7 m target.

Bands covered

  • 905 nm — silicon detector, 1–20 g, 9 m to 3 km, up to 1 kHz
  • 940 nm — single-point LiDAR, 9–50 m, 1.0–1.5 g
  • 1535 nm — erbium glass + InGaAs, 3–23 km, 14–410 g, 0.5–10 Hz
